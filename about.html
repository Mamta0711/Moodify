About Moodify

Moodify is a mood-based music playlist generator built to lift your spirits, calm your mind, energize your day, and accompany you through every emotion. We fetch curated playlists from Spotify and bring the perfect vibe based on how you're feeling. 🎶

Whether you're feeling happy, nostalgic, focused, or romantic, Moodify has the right soundtrack for you!

Our Mission

To make music accessible, personal, and mood-enhancing for everyone, everywhere.

Our Vision

Empowering emotional well-being through the power of personalized soundtracks.

About the Creator

This project was developed as a creative fusion of web development and music appreciation. Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Spotify's API.
